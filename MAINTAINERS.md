## Maintainer principles

Long Gate maintainers should prefer:

- fail-closed behavior over convenience;
- explicit threat-model assumptions over vague “privacy scores”;
- reproducible evidence over security marketing;
- adapters over unnecessary reinvention;
- narrow capabilities over prompt-only restrictions;
- transparent limitations over premature certification claims.
